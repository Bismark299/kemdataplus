# Agent Payout Rules & Guidelines

## 📋 Overview

This document outlines the rules, requirements, and procedures for agent profit payouts on the KemDataPlus platform. All agents must adhere to these guidelines to ensure smooth and timely processing of their earnings.

---

## 💰 Earning Profits

### How Profits Are Earned
- Profits are generated from **store sales** made through your personalized storefront
- Each successful order contributes profit to your "Pending Earnings"
- Profits appear in your wallet under **"Available to Withdraw"**

### Profit Tracking
| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Today's Earnings | Profits from orders completed today |
| Available to Withdraw | Total accumulated profits ready for withdrawal |
| Total Paid | Historical total of successfully paid out profits |

---

## 📝 Withdrawal Requirements

### Minimum Withdrawal Amount
- **Minimum: GH₵ 5.00** (after processing fees)
- Requests below this threshold will be rejected

### Processing Fee
- **Flat fee: GH₵ 0.50** per withdrawal transaction
- This fee is deducted from your withdrawal amount
- Example: Request GH₵10 → Receive GH₵9.50

### MoMo Account Requirements
1. **Verification Required**: Your MoMo number must be verified before withdrawal
2. **Supported Networks**:
   - MTN Mobile Money
   - Vodafone Cash / Telecel Cash
   - AirtelTigo Money
3. **Account Name Match**: The MoMo account name will be auto-detected during verification
4. **Save Details**: You can save your MoMo details for future withdrawals

## ⏰ Payout Schedule

### Standard Processing
| Day | Time | Activity |
|-----|------|----------|
| **Friday** | **7:30 PM (19:30) Ghana Time** | Weekly batch payout processing |

### Processing Timeline
1. **Request Submission**: Available any time during the week
2. **Request Accumulation**: Multiple requests within the same period are combined
3. **Batch Processing**: All pending requests processed every Friday evening
4. **Completion**: Funds arrive in MoMo within minutes after processing

## 🔄 Withdrawal Status Flow

Your withdrawal request goes through these stages:

```
PENDING → RESERVED → PROCESSING → COMPLETED
                 ↘               ↙
                    FAILED / REJECTED
```

### Status Explanations

| Status | Description | Can Cancel? |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **PENDING** | Request submitted, awaiting processing | ✅ Yes |
| **RESERVED** | Funds locked, preparing for transfer | ❌ No |
| **PROCESSING** | Transfer in progress via MoMo | ❌ No |
| **COMPLETED** | Successfully transferred to your MoMo | N/A |
| **FAILED** | Transfer failed (funds restored to earnings) | N/A |
| **REJECTED** | Request rejected by admin | N/A |

---

## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. One Active Request at a Time
- You can only have **one PENDING request** at a time
- Additional withdrawal requests **accumulate** to your existing pending request
- You **cannot** submit new requests while a withdrawal is being processed (RESERVED/PROCESSING)

### 2. Request Accumulation
When you submit additional withdrawal requests while one is pending:
- Your requests are **combined** into a single payout
- The fee remains **GH₵ 0.50** (single transaction)
- Updated MoMo details from the latest request are used

### 3. Cancellation Policy
- **Can cancel**: Requests in PENDING status only
- **Cannot cancel**: Once status changes to RESERVED or PROCESSING
- Cancelled requests restore funds to your available earnings

### 4. Failed Transfers
If a transfer fails:
- Your funds are **automatically restored** to available earnings
- You will receive notification of the failure
- You can submit a new withdrawal request

## 📱 Submission Process

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Navigate to Wallet** → Select "Pending Earnings" tab
2. **Select MoMo Network** (MTN, Vodafone/Telecel, AirtelTigo)
3. **Enter MoMo Number** (10 digits, e.g., 0240000000)
4. **Click "Verify"** to confirm account details
5. **Enter Amount** (minimum GH₵5 after fee)
6. **Review Fee Breakdown**
7. **Submit Request**

### Verification Requirements
- MoMo number must be verified before each withdrawal
- Account name is auto-populated from verification
- Ensure account details are accurate to avoid failed transfers

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: When will I receive my money?
**A:** Payouts are processed every **Friday at 7:30 PM Ghana time**. Once processed, funds arrive within minutes.

### Q: Can I withdraw to someone else's MoMo?
**A:** You can withdraw to any verified MoMo number, but the account name will be verified and recorded.

### Q: What happens if my withdrawal fails?
**A:** Your funds are automatically restored to your available earnings. Check your MoMo details and try again.

### Q: Why can't I cancel my withdrawal?
**A:** Once your request enters RESERVED or PROCESSING status, it's being prepared for transfer and cannot be cancelled to prevent duplicate payouts.

### Q: Can I change the withdrawal amount after submission?
**A:** Submit an additional request while your original is still PENDING - they will be accumulated.

### Q: Is there a maximum withdrawal amount?
**A:** No maximum, but you can only withdraw your **available earnings balance**.

### Q: Why was my withdrawal rejected?
**A:** Common reasons:
- Invalid MoMo details
- Insufficient available earnings
- Account verification failure
- Admin discretion (with reason provided)
